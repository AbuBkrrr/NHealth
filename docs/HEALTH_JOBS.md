# Health jobs (gigs)

Verified providers recruit health workers; health workers find jobs and shifts.

## Employers
**Who can post:** verified hospitals and clinics, pharmacies, diagnostic centers, ambulance services, and doctors or nurses with a private practice.

**Where:** Home → Health jobs → **Post a job**.

**The "Who are you recruiting?" list depends on the provider:**

| Provider | Can recruit |
|---|---|
| Hospital / clinic | Doctor, specialist, nurse, midwife, pharmacist, pharmacy technician, lab scientist/technician, radiographer, sonographer, physiotherapist, dentist, optometrist, anaesthetist, dietitian, health records officer, community health worker, caregiver, paramedic, ambulance driver, front desk, administrator |
| Pharmacy | Pharmacist, pharmacy technician, sales/dispensing assistant, dispatch rider, front desk/cashier, administrator |
| Diagnostic center | Lab scientist, lab technician, radiographer, sonographer, phlebotomist, specialist (e.g. pathologist/radiologist), dispatch rider, front desk, administrator |
| Ambulance service | Paramedic/EMT, nurse, doctor, ambulance driver, dispatcher |
| Doctor's practice | Doctor, nurse, midwife, pharmacist, lab technician, caregiver, front desk |
| Nurse / home-care agency | Nurse, midwife, caregiver |

**The rest of the form:**
- number needed
- experience level
- full-time, part-time, locum, contract or internship
- shifts and hours
- start and end dates
- pay range and period, in naira, and whether it's negotiable
- licence required
- requirements and benefits
- location
- closing date
- urgent

**After posting:**
- **Alerts:** health workers who are open to work in that profession and state are alerted at once.
- **Reviewing applicants:** each applicant shows their profession, experience, licence, ID and licence-verified badges, expected pay, availability, note and CV.
- **Moving applicants along:** Shortlist, Invite to interview, Make offer, Hired or Not selected. Each step notifies the applicant, by SMS for interview, offer and hired.
- **Contact details:** the applicant's phone and email appear once they are shortlisted.
- **Filling and closing:** a job fills itself when enough people are hired. Closing it tells everyone still waiting.

## Health workers
**Who:** anyone can look for work, including doctors and nurses on N-Health and people registered as patients, such as pharmacists, lab scientists and caregivers.

**How:**
1. **Set up a work profile once:** profession, licence, experience, skills, CV, job types, states, expected pay, open to work, job alerts.
2. **Find jobs:** filter by profession, state and job type.
3. **Apply in one tap** and follow each application under **My applications**.

## Safety
- Only verified providers can post.
- N-Health never charges job seekers, and posts that ask applicants for money are removed.
- Anyone can **report a job**. A job is paused automatically after 5 reports, and admins review reported jobs in Admin console → **Health jobs**.
